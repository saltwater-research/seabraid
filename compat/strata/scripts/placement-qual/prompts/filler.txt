The east pier was repainted in the second week of the month, and the crew noted that the new coating dried faster than the old one, though the color was slightly lighter than expected.

A delivery of forty crates arrived on the morning tide; thirty-one held ceramic tiles, six held brass hinges, and three were found to be empty on inspection.

The weather station on the hill recorded a steady wind from the northwest, with gusts reaching moderate strength in the afternoon and a calm, clear evening.

Two apprentices were assigned to catalogue the spare parts shelf, sorting bolts by length and washers by thickness, and labeling each drawer in neat block letters.

The ferry timetable was revised so that the last crossing leaves twenty minutes earlier in winter, a change posted on the notice board beside the ticket window.

An inspector walked the length of the warehouse and signed the safety sheet, remarking that the fire extinguishers were in place and the aisles were clear.

The bakery on the corner extended its hours on market days, and the line of customers often reached the doorway of the hardware store next door.

A broken lamp on the third landing was replaced; the electrician mentioned that the wiring in that stairwell is older than the building's current roof.
